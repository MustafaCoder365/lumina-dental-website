Common questions

Will I know the price before treatment?

Yes. After the exam you get a written plan with the cost of each step, and we only start with your approval.

Does it hurt?

We use local anesthesia for anything that could be painful, and we stop whenever you ask.

Can I pay in installments?

For implants and aligners, the cost can be split across treatment stages. We agree on this in your written plan.

From what age do you see children?

From the first tooth. We recommend a first visit before age two.

What should I do in an emergency?

Call us during opening hours and we'll find you the earliest same-day slot we can.
